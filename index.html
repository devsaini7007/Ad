The Systems

Everything your
business needs.
Automated.

Jarvis is a complete operating system — not a single tool. Every system works together, all driven by one AI that knows your business.

📞
Lead Follow-Up AI

Jarvis calls, texts, and emails your leads on the right cadence. It qualifies them, handles objections, and flags hot prospects for you to close.

📅
Viewing Scheduler

Clients book property viewings directly through Jarvis. It syncs to your calendar, sends confirmations, and follows up with prospects who don't show.

✍️
Listing Copy Writer

Give Jarvis the property details and it writes a compelling, SEO-optimised listing description in seconds. Portals, social media, and email — all covered.

🧠
Morning Pipeline Brief

Every morning, Jarvis gives you a voice or text briefing: who to call, which deals are moving, and which leads need attention today.

⭐
Review Collector

After every completed transaction, Jarvis automatically requests a Google review from your client. Build your reputation without lifting a finger.

🔁
Past Client Reactivation

Jarvis re-engages your dormant database with personalised outreach — market updates, property matches, check-ins — turning old contacts into new deals.

💬
Inbound Enquiry Handler

Every portal enquiry, website form, and social DM gets an instant, intelligent response from Jarvis — 24/7. No lead ever waits for a reply again.

🏡
Property Matching AI

Jarvis cross-matches your live listings against your buyer database and proactively notifies the right prospects when a matching property comes on the market.

📊
Deal Intelligence

A real-time view of your entire pipeline — deal stages, follow-up gaps, revenue forecasts, and conversion rates — surfaced in plain language when you ask.
